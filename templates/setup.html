Configuration Tips
Sensors
  • Temperature: DS18B20 (1-Wire), GPIO pin 4 is common
  • Pressure: I2C sensors, specify hex address
  • Flow: GPIO interrupt pins for pulse counting
Devices
  • Heaters: Relay control, use GPIO pins 17, 27, 22
  • Pumps: Relay or direct control for circulation
  • Valves: Solenoid valves for liquid flow control
